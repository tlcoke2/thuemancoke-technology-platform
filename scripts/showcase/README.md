# TCL software and website showcase

Owner-authorised public portfolio film. Silent AI-assisted motion graphics; no simulated team footage, clinical records, private portals or compliance-certification claims. Public website captures: https://healthyconceptmedical.com/ and https://www.gordoncolegal.com/. Captures show business websites, not real patients or clients; the healthcare illustration was already labelled illustrative on its source website.

34 seconds, 1280x720, 20 fps, H.264/yuv420p, fast-start MP4, no audio. The player is first-party, controls-only and preload=none. On-screen text, an English WebVTT track and a page transcript support access without sound. No third-party video player, tracking embed or autoplay.

To regenerate with Pillow, DejaVu Sans and ffmpeg installed: `python scripts/showcase/render.py`. Owner-authorised full-resolution captures may be supplied as healthy-source.jpg and gordon-source.jpg next to the renderer. Otherwise it uses the retained public WebP website stills. A fresh rerender may have different file hashes; keep the published file and review replacements before publication.

Scenes: 0-4s brand introduction; 4-10s illustrated build process; 10-16s TEC EPR workflow illustration; 16-22s Healthy Concept website; 22-28s Gordon & Co website; 28-34s website/software enquiry call to action.

The original software archives, customer gateway, licences, prices, database volumes and payment settings are outside this change.
